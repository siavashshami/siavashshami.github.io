## Research Profile
<p style="text-align: justify; text-justify: inter-ideograph; direction: ltr; width: 100%;">
I am a geodesist and InSAR researcher specializing in SAR-based surface deformation monitoring and Earth observation. My research focuses on InSAR methodology, interferometric time-series analysis, network design, and error characterization, with an emphasis on reliable deformation measurements from satellite radar observations. I integrate InSAR with GNSS, remote sensing, geological, and hydrological data to investigate natural and anthropogenic deformation. I also apply machine learning and deep learning approaches to deformation modeling and environmental remote sensing.
</p>
<strong>Research Interests</strong><br>
•	InSAR and SAR Remote Sensing<br>
•	Surface and Crustal Deformation Monitoring<br>
•	InSAR Time-Series Analysis, Network Design, and Error Characterization<br>
•	Geohazards and Natural & Anthropogenic Deformation<br>
•	Environmental and Climate Applications of Remote Sensing<br>
•	Machine Learning and Deep Learning for Geodesy and Earth Observation
