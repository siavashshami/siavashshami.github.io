## About
<p style="text-align: justify; text-justify: inter-ideograph; direction: ltr; width: 100%;">
Siavash Shami is a geodesist and InSAR researcher specializing in SAR-based surface deformation monitoring and Earth observation. His research focuses on InSAR methodology, interferometric time-series analysis, error characterization, and the integration of InSAR with GNSS, remote sensing, geological, and hydrological data to investigate natural and anthropogenic deformation. He also applies machine learning and deep learning to deformation modeling and environmental remote sensing.
</p>

## Research Interests
- InSAR and SAR Remote Sensing
- Surface and Crustal Deformation Monitoring
- InSAR Time-Series Analysis, Network Design, and Error Characterization
- Geohazards and Natural & Anthropogenic Deformation
- Remote Sensing for Environmental and Climate Change Studies
- Machine Learning and Deep Learning for Earth Science Applications
