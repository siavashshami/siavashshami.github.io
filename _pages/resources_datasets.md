All data are provided in standard GIS‑compatible formats (CSV, Shapefile) for integrated spatial analysis and deformation modeling in this subsiding region.

    2. Hashtgerd Plain Dataset (InSAR, GNSS, Hydrological & Geological Data)
    <span style="margin-left: auto; font-size: 0.9rem; color: #888; font-weight: 400;">
      ⬇️ Click to expand

  <div style="padding-top: 1rem; border-top: 1px solid #eee; margin-top: 0.8rem;">

    <p style="font-size: 1rem; line-height: 1.7; color: #444;">
      <strong>📌 Description:</strong><br>
This comprehensive dataset compiles multi‑source geospatial and deformation measurements for the Hashtgerd plain, Iran, to support integrated analysis of land subsidence and its driving factors. It includes Sentinel‑1 InSAR displacement time‑series (ascending and descending) processed using NSBAS, GNSS observations for independent validation and velocity estimation, piezometric well data for groundwater level monitoring, and landcover classification layers. In addition, the dataset provides key geological and hydrogeological shapefiles, including Hashtgerd aquifer boundaries, basin extents, geoelectric sections and borehole logs, and the regional Alborz geology map. All data are provided in standard GIS‑compatible formats (CSV, GeoTIFF, Shapefile), enabling multi‑layered spatial analysis of subsidence patterns, groundwater dynamics, lithological controls, and landcover influences for hazard assessment and water‑resource management in this subsiding region.
</p>
